Case File No. 001

The incident that started it all

This project did not begin as a joke. It began as a near miss.

FIELD REPORT — INDUSTRIAL VISIT, CLASS TRIP

A classmate was sitting under a coconut tree during a college industrial visit, watching a few others take photos nearby. No one was looking up. No one ever does.

Then, without warning: boing.

A coconut came down directly onto her head. The only thing standing between her and a genuinely serious injury was the hat she happened to be wearing that day. The hat did not survive. She did.

Verdict: the hat is a hero. This website exists because a piece of headwear took a hit that a person otherwise would have.

How the system works

From tree photo to threat report

1. Upload or capture

Point a camera at a coconut bunch, or upload a photo of one.

2. Locate every coconut

A YOLOv11 model trained specifically on coconut bunches draws a bounding box around each individual coconut.

3. Classify ripeness

Each detected coconut is cropped and passed to a maturity classification model: Mature, Potential, or Premature.

4. Score the threat

Size, maturity, and height combine into a lethality score, translated into a threat level for each coconut.

5. File the paperwork

High threat coconuts are logged into the crime registry, added to the Most Wanted board, or cleared with an official Tree Safety Certificate.
